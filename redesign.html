Questions about redesigns

Will I lose my Google rankings?

We redirect your old page addresses to the new ones and carry over your page titles, which is the standard way to protect them. Rankings can still move for a while after any redesign, and nobody can honestly promise otherwise.

Do I keep my domain?

Yes. You keep your domain name, and your customers keep finding you at the same address.

Will my site go down during the switch?

We build the new site separately, and your old one stays up until you've approved the new one. The switch itself usually goes unnoticed.

Can you use my existing text and photos?

Yes. We reuse what's good and rewrite what isn't. Tell us if anything has to stay word for word.

What if my site is on WordPress or Wix?

That's fine. We rebuild it as a new site, so it doesn't matter what it runs on now. Tell us if you'd rather stay on your current platform, and we'll tell you if we can.

Are domain and hosting included?

No. Domain and hosting are not included in the plan price and are charged to the client. We'll tell you the exact cost before you approve anything.

Questions owners ask

Is the preview really free?

Yes. You get one free preview per business, and we don't ask for a card. You only pay if you approve it.

What if I don't like the preview?

Tell us what's wrong and we'll tell you whether we can fix it. You can also just walk away. You won't owe anything.

When do I pay, and how?

You pay 50% when you approve the preview and 50% before the site goes live. We send an invoice link for each payment.

Who owns the website?

You do, once it's paid for. Your domain is registered in your name, and we hand over your site files whenever you ask.

Are domain and hosting included?

No. Domain and hosting are not included in the plan price and are charged to the client. We'll tell you the exact cost before you approve anything.

Can you get me to the top of Google?

We can't promise that, and you should be careful of anyone who does. We set your site up properly so Google can understand what you do and where you work.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. There's a one-page agreement covering the work, payment, refund and ownership. Nothing in it locks you in month to month.

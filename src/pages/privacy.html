Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who we are

MarveloWeb builds websites for US local service businesses, and we work from India. Contact us at noah@marveloweb.com.

What we collect

When you request a preview, the form opens your own email app with your request filled in, and we receive what you choose to send: your name, business name, trade, town, email, phone, website and chosen plan.

To build a preview, we also use public details about your business, such as your business listing, website and reviews.

How we use it

We use your details to build your preview, contact you about it, and deliver and bill any work you approve. We don't use them for anything else without asking you.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your details. We share them only with the services we need to run our business:

  • Google, which handles our email and serves this site's fonts.
  • GitHub, which hosts this site, and Cloudflare, which runs its domain settings.
  • Unsplash, which serves stock photos on this site.
  • Our payment provider, if you hire us. It handles your card details, and we don't see or store them.

Our hosting, font and photo services can see technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads this site. We may also share details if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set its own cookies, and it doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers. If we add tools such as Google Analytics or Google Ads, we'll update this policy first and explain how to opt out.

How long we keep it

We keep requests for 1 year and then delete them, unless you hire us. If you do, we keep project and billing records for as long as the law requires.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your details, email noah@marveloweb.com. If we email you first, you can reply "no thanks" and we won't contact you again.

Where your details are handled

We work from India, and the services above may process details in other countries, including the United States.

Security

This site uses HTTPS. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so please don't send passwords or card numbers by email.

Children

Our services are for businesses and aren't directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
